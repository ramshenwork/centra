About CENTRA

CENTRA provides booking and business-management software to independent businesses. Customers book directly with each business; CENTRA is not a party to the service the business provides.

Bookings, closures and contact

  • If a business closes on a day that already has bookings, the business contacts its customers directly, and customers can call the business on the number shown on its booking page. CENTRA is not responsible for a business closing on a booked day.
  • Phone numbers entered by customers are not verified. A wrong or fake number is the customer's responsibility.

Deposits and cancellations

  • Each business decides whether to take a deposit, its percentage, and the cancellation window for a full refund. These are shown before payment.
  • Cancelling at least the stated number of hours before the appointment refunds the deposit in full. Later cancellations forfeit the deposit. If the business cancels, the deposit is refunded.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay.

Business accounts

  • Plans are billed in advance for the chosen period. Launch prices are shown as a starting offer and may change for later periods.
  • After repeated incorrect sign-in attempts, an account is locked for a period that doubles with each further lock.

Privacy

Customer details are used only to manage bookings with the business they booked with.
